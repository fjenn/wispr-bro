Takes went through the running test app's real record path: SIGUSR2
(Handy's toggle) → microphone → Parakeet → text pipeline → `fork::insert`
(S-05 target check) → ⌘V / typing or the card. Speech: one `say -v
Samantha` WAV ("Please send the report to the team before lunch, and copy me
on the reply."), played through the speakers into the built-in mic. Every
take held `~/wb-bench/gui.lock`, opened its own window, checked the
frontmost app before recording **and again right before the stop** (if
another app had taken the front, the take was cancelled with `handy
--cancel`, see below), and closed what it opened; card takes saved the
whole clipboard first and put it back afterwards. Procedure:
`scripts/wb-s05/run.py <out> 1` (MAC-TEST-SCRIPT S-05). Raw results stay on
the Mac (scratch folder of the run, never committed).

After this run S-05 was rebased onto S-07a: its card became S-07a's card
(one state, the reason as the label; unit and Playwright tests updated) and
the `WISPRBRO_TEST_PILL` switch was removed (test identities now run as
product builds). The routes and the AX layer did not change, so no new
speaker run was made; the card's look on the Mac after the merge was not
re-checked.

## Incident

In one earlier take (before the stop-time front check existed) another
agent's AntiChat dev window took the front during the recording; the take
was delivered there (route `paste (fail-open)`, `AXGroup`/`AXHostingView`,
`unverified`). The test sentence above may have been pasted into that
AntiChat window. `take.sh` now checks the frontmost app right before the
stop and cancels the take otherwise; after key-up the product itself sends
the text to the card if the focus moves.

## Safari

No paste reached `fields.html` in Safari on this Mac today, from any
process: `osascript … keystroke "v" using command down` with Safari
frontmost and the field focused also left the field empty
(`scripts/wb-s05/safari-paste.sh`). Wispr Bro's paste was `unverified` and
Safari never read the transcript from the clipboard. S-11 pasted into
Safari earlier on the same Mac, so this is the session (Safari or the
shared GUI), not the slice. S-05's criterion (no card with a text field
focused) holds: every Safari field got the right verdict.
