# S-07b real-Mac run: silent model policy, upgrades and cleanup

- **Build:** `1.0.0-alpha.1` debug, `--no-bundle`, test identity
  `com.wisprbro.s07bcheck` (a product build through
  `fork::is_test_identity`), portable mode (`portable` file next to the
  binary, data in `Data/`).
- **Machine:** M-26 (M5 Max, 40-core GPU, 128 GB, macOS 26.6.2). Other
  agents shared the Mac; GUI and speaker steps held `~/wb-bench/gui.lock`.
- **Network:** model files from `scripts/wb-s07a/hf-mirror.py` (loopback
  stand-in for Hugging Face serving `~/wb-bench/asr`) through
  `HF_ENDPOINT`. The bench files were downloaded once from the pinned
  `handy-computer/*` repositories on `huggingface.co` and checked against
  the catalog's sha256.

## 1. Smaller starters for slow links (S-07a open item a)

S-07a measured 134 s from launch to "ready" at 10 Mbit/s in English: the
135 MB parakeet-110m Q8_0 file alone is 108 s, against §5.3's 90 s. The
RS-09 bench (`bun scripts/wb-bench-asr.ts bench`, the owner's reading
script, M5 Max, Metal; the quants added to `MODELS` in
`scripts/wb-bench-asr.ts`, files at the catalog's pinned revisions) on the
smaller quants:

French and Dutch short takes on whisper-base: Q8_0 28.3 % / 63.6 %, Q5_K_M
26.9 % / 50.8 %, Q4_K_M 33.8 % / 62.1 % (Dutch stays unusable on every
quant, DEC-52).

**Decision.** The English starter is parakeet-110m **Q4_K_M** on every
link (no link-speed probe is needed): +2 errors in 138 words on short
takes (11 vs 9) and slightly
better on the long takes, all within one speaker's noise and far under the
15 % starter gate, still better than whisper-base; its transfer at
10 Mbit/s is 72 s, inside §5.3's 90 s. The main model follows in the
background anyway (parakeet-unified-en on every Apple Silicon class), so
the starter is a stopgap of minutes. Q4_K_M is not a catalog default, so
Handy's downloader cannot fetch it; the fork's own transfer
(`setup::transfer_alternate`, hf-hub into the same cache at the pinned
revision, then Handy's rescan registers it) does, and the checksum check
and retry machine are unchanged. whisper-base stays at Q8_0 for French and
the other Whisper languages: 85 MB already transfers in 68 s, and Q4_K_M
is slower on Metal and worse in French.

Raw results (transcripts of the owner's voice) stay in
`~/wb-bench/results/rs09/bench/` and were not copied.

src-tauri/tauri.fork.conf.json --config <scratch>/s07b.conf.json`, the
binary with its `resources/` in a scratch `target/debug/` (with
`.cargo-lock`, so Tauri resolves resources next to it) plus a `portable`
file. Stand-in on :18177. Driver: `scripts/wb-s07b/run.sh`. Seed settings:
onboarding done, `spoken_languages` set by the run.

**Seen on the way:** in the 10 Mbit/s run the new model's engine worker did
not answer its hello within 60 s (the Mac was stalling every new process
launch for minutes at a time during this session, also `cargo`, `bun`,
`node`); Handy's supervisor killed it and the second worker loaded the
model after 113 s. The old model stayed selected and on disk until then,
then the swap and deletion happened as designed.
