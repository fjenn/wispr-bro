Procedure: MAC-TEST-SCRIPT S-06 (`scripts/wb-s06/setup.py`, then
`scripts/wb-s06/case.sh <case>` once per case). Every case held
`~/wb-bench/gui.lock`, opened its own window (a new TextEdit document, a
separate Chrome instance on `scripts/wb-s05/fields.html`, a Terminal window
running `cat` into a file), posted each synthetic event (`post.swift`, at
the HID tap) only while that window's app was frontmost, saved the whole
clipboard first and checked it byte-identical after, and closed what it
opened. Paste-last and copy-last used a seeded History dictation with a fixed
text ("Recovery check: the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.", 60
characters); no speakers. The three Undo cases used one `say` WAV ("Please
bring this sentence back after I cancel it by mistake.") played once per case
through the speakers into the built-in mic (output volume left at the
owner's 50 %, not changed). Raw results stay on the Mac (scratch folder of
the run, never committed).

## Not run, and why

- **Slack and Mail** are not set up on this Mac for unattended use (the
  owner's own apps and accounts are off limits): Chrome stood in for an
  Electron/Chromium field and TextEdit for Mail's compose window. Pasting
  into the owner's Claude or Cursor windows (the S-04 stand-ins) was not
  done: they are not windows this run opened.
- **A real mouse's side button**: none attached; the back button was a
  synthetic `otherMouseDown` at the HID tap (RS-11 showed the same path).
  The middle-click-also-opens-a-link note was not checked in a browser.
- **A cancel during "Transcribing…"** (Undo reuses Handy's WAV): the
  transcription of a short take is ~100 ms, too short to cancel into
  reliably from a script; covered by the unit tests (`recovery` in-flight
  bookkeeping, `undo::plan`).
- **The tap-with-no-second-press and DEC-54 chord cases** (no offer): unit
  and engine tests only; the run's takes were started with Handy's toggle.
- **VoiceOver** on the offer, **Esc and ✕ on the offer** (dismiss drops the
  audio): Playwright and unit tests only.
- **The Recovery shortcuts section** recording a shortcut through Handy's
  recorder in the real app: Playwright with mocked recorder events only.
