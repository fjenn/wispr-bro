### The app in the background (`WISPRBRO_SELFCHECK_APPLE=1`)

App started with `WB_DEBUG_LLM_HOLD_MODEL=1 WISPRBRO_SELFCHECK_APPLE=1
./handy --start-hidden` (never in front): the self-check ran the app's own
`plan` + `run` (engine choice, key-down prewarm, guards, record, event) on
our ten 50-word samples, 8 takes after 20 s idle with the prewarm and 1.5 s
of "speech", then 30 back to back:

```text
fork apple selfcheck: availability Available, role Fallback, context 4096 tokens
cleanup: applied light (apple, 1/1 chunk(s), 1119 ms, 0 fix(es))
…
fork apple selfcheck: PASS engines {"apple": 38} outcomes {"applied": 35, "guard": 3}; prewarmed p50/p95 (1227, 2225) ms, burst p50/p95 (1405, 1535) ms; requests 38 refusals 0 rate_limited 0
```

All 38 takes ran on Apple's model with the local model held "not ready",
**no `rateLimited` while the app was a background app**, no refusal; the
three guard rejections went in as the deterministic text. The 2225 ms p95 is
the first take, whose prewarm had no earlier instructions to reuse.

## S-12b.2: real dictations with Apple's model standing in

App restarted with `WB_DEBUG_LLM_HOLD_MODEL=1` only. `scripts/wb-s12b/takes.sh`:
three WAVs made once with `say -o` (Samantha en, Xander nl), played through
the speakers at output volume 40 (restored after) into the built-in mic,
each take through S-12a's `take.sh` (SIGUSR2 → record → Parakeet → text
pipeline → cleanup → ⌘V into a new TextEdit document the script made and
closed, under `~/wb-bench/gui.lock`). The general pasteboard was saved and
restored around the run (`clip.swift`, digest `d8bfbc5c…` before and after).

| Take        | Key-up → text | Cleanup (log)                                           | Inserted                                                                             |
| ----------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| en_fillers  | 1173 ms       | `guard light (apple, …, 955 ms, guard: content_words)`  | "I was thinking we could, meet on Friday, no wait, Thursday, to go over the budget." |
| en_question | 142 ms        | `skipped light (none, already_clean)`                   | "What is the capital of Australia?"                                                  |
| nl_repair   | 2129 ms       | `guard light (apple, …, 1183 ms, guard: content_words)` | "Huh, we spreken af om drie uur, nee wacht om vier uur."                             |

Both requests ran on Apple's model (`entry_meta`: `light|apple|guard`) and
were rejected by the content-word guard, so the deterministic text went in
(the S-12a fail-open, with the "Inserted without cleanup" notice); the
question needed no LLM. In the background self-check the same model applied
35 of 38 50-word takes; the 3-take sample here is too small to say more than
that the real dictation path reaches Apple's model, records it and fails
open. Only these three takes used the speakers.

## Not verified

- Apple Intelligence off or an ineligible Mac (S-12b.4): no such Mac here,
  and the setting on this Mac was not changed. Covered by unit tests (the
  availability mapping, the stub build with `WB_APPLE_FM_STUB=1`) and the
  Playwright spec (every reason's text).
- The memory-rule fallback on a real tight Mac (M1 8 GB): only the decision
  table is unit-tested.
- A real AI-chat app, a person's voice, other languages on real speech
  beyond one Dutch take.
- macOS 26.0-26.3 and macOS 27 at runtime (the bridge maps macOS 27's
  `LanguageModelError`, compiled against the 27.0 SDK, not run there).
- The machine was shared: process launches stalled for 10-15 minutes at a
  time during the session (all agents' builds waited in `_dyld_start`);
  latencies are from runs between the stalls.
