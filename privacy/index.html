Privacy policy

Wispr Bro for iPhone and Mac. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Short version: Wispr Bro collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your voice and your text stay on your device.

Your voice and text

Speech is turned into text on your device. Audio is processed in memory and is not uploaded anywhere. Your dictations, dictionary, snippets, styles and Scratchpad notes are stored only on your device.

Speech models

On first launch the app downloads its speech model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This is a plain file download: no audio, text or personal information is sent. Hugging Face, like any web server, sees your IP address when the file is requested.

Cleanup with Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, optional text cleanup uses Apple's on-device model. It runs on your iPhone; Wispr Bro does not send your text to Apple or anyone else.

Sync between your devices

If you turn on sync, your iPhone and your Mac exchange data directly with each other over your local network. Nothing goes through a server run by us or anyone else.

Keyboard and Full Access

The Wispr Bro keyboard asks for Full Access only so it can exchange the dictated text with the Wispr Bro app on the same device. The keyboard does not log what you type and makes no network requests. Typing works without Full Access; only dictation needs it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you dictate, and only in the Wispr Bro app.

Children

Wispr Bro collects no data from anyone, including children.
