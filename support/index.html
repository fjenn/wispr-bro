Set up the keyboard on iPhone

  1. Open Settings › Wispr Bro › Keyboards and turn on Wispr Bro.
  2. Turn on Allow Full Access (needed for dictation only; typing works without it).
  3. In any text field, hold the globe key and choose Wispr Bro.

Dictating

Tap the microphone on the keyboard. The first time, Wispr Bro opens for a moment to start listening, because iOS only lets the app itself use the microphone. Swipe back to the app you were in and talk.

First launch

The first launch downloads the speech model (about 700 MB). After that, dictation works offline.
